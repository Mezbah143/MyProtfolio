Hotel Booking System

A full-stack Spring Boot application that turns hotel discovery, booking, and administration into one dependable workflow.

View source ↗Back to work

The problem

Hotel staff and guests need different workflows: guests need fast room discovery and booking confirmation, while admins need control over rooms, users, and reservations.

What I built

  • Registration, login, logout, and user/admin roles.
  • Room search by keyword, city, type, guests, price, and dates.
  • Booking creation, confirmation, cancellation, and admin management.
  • Persistent room image uploads backed by MySQL.

What I learned

I practiced connecting server-rendered views to Spring Data JPA, keeping role-specific actions clear, and configuring a deployable MySQL-backed application.
